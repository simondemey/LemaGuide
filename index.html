About me

Hello, I'm Lema

Welcome to LeJaSi Africa Adventure!

My name is Lema and I am a professional Mountain and Safari Guide based in Tanzania, with over 10 years of experience in adventure tourism.

My passion is introducing visitors from around the world to the extraordinary beauty of Tanzania, from the breathtaking peaks of Mount Kilimanjaro, Mount Meru and the active volcano Ol Doinyo Lengai to the incredible wildlife and natural landscapes of Africa.

Throughout my guiding career, I have developed valuable experience in mountain trekking, wildlife safaris, guest care, and creating unforgettable travel experiences.

As a local Tanzanian guide, I believe that every journey should be safe, enjoyable, authentic, and memorable. I am committed to providing professional guidance, personal attention, and warm Tanzanian hospitality to every guest.

Whether your dream is to reach the summit of Mount Kilimanjaro, explore the wildlife of Serengeti National Park, discover the beauty of Ngorongoro Crater, or experience Tanzania’s rich culture, I am here to make your dream adventure a reality.
