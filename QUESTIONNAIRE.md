# What I need for each part of the website

**1. Top of the page**
- Text: a short slogan (one line)
- Photo: 1 beautiful photo of Kilimanjaro (wide, landscape)

**2. About me**
- Text: your story in 3–5 sentences
- Photo: 1 photo of yourself

**3. Routes**
- Text: for each route, the name, number of days and 2 sentences about it
- Photo: 1 photo for each route

**4. Photos**
- Photo: 5–10 photos from the mountain (summit, camps, team, guests)

**5. Reviews**
- Text: for each review, the guest's name, country, month and what they wrote
- Photo: a photo of the guest (optional)

**6. Contact**
- Text: your email address

Please send photos on WhatsApp as a "Document" to keep the quality good.
